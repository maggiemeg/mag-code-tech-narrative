Code and digital technology impact on education

Code and digital technology has transformed not only our classrooms, but our cirriculum as we know it from primary to secondary education and beyond. Now with AI and computers readily accessible for young students, they are lacking hands-on learning and key development opportunities that are limiting later on in their education. It baffles me that many children entering high school and even higher level education can't write an essay or perform on paper exams. While I think developing our digital literacies is crucial to enter the professional landscape, it is as if not more important that we are still experienced in challenging, cognitive skills. I love being able to type and collaborate on an online space as much as I love getting to discuss ideas with my peers in person. Finding that appropriate balance is key for all generations.
